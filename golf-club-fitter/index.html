📏 Golf Club Length Scanner

Stand in front of your camera and we'll scan your body to estimate your wrist-to-floor measurement — then recommend a close-enough club length, all in your browser.

Step 1 · Your height

The scanner reads your body proportions from the camera, but it needs your height to turn those proportions into real inches. Nothing is uploaded — detection runs entirely on your device.

Stand straight in shoes, arms relaxed at your sides, and measure from the crease of your wrist straight down to the floor.

Step 2 · Body scan

  • Put your phone/laptop down and step back so your whole body is in frame.
  • Face the camera, stand up straight, feet flat.
  • Let your arms hang relaxed at your sides — don't hold them out.
